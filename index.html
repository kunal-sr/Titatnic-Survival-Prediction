ML pipeline — 9 steps

main.py
1

Data loading & concatenation

Train (891 rows) and test (418 rows) CSVs are loaded and concatenated into a single dataframe to ensure consistent preprocessing across both splits.

pd.read_csv("train.csv")pd.concat([train, test])

2

Title extraction from name

Titles are extracted via regex from passenger names (e.g., "Mr", "Mrs", "Miss"). Rare titles — Lady, Countess, Capt, Dr, Rev, Sir etc. — are consolidated into a single "Rare" bucket. Mlle/Ms → Miss, Mme → Mrs.

str.extract(r" ([A-Za-z]+)\.")replace(rare_titles, "Rare")

3

Smart age imputation

Missing age values (177 in train) are filled using the median age within each title group. "Master" passengers receive a child-appropriate median while "Mr" receives an adult median — far more accurate than a global fill.

groupby("Title")["Age"].transform(lambda x: x.fillna(x.median()))

4

Remaining missing values

Fare is filled with the global median (1 missing value in the test set). Embarked port is filled with the mode "S" — 2 missing values in training data.

Fare.fillna(median)Embarked.fillna(mode)

5

Categorical encoding

Title is one-hot encoded. Sex is label-encoded (male=0, female=1). Embarked is one-hot encoded. All encodings use drop_first=True to prevent multicollinearity in the feature matrix.

pd.get_dummies(columns=["Title"], drop_first=True)Sex.map({"male":0,"female":1})

6

Feature engineering

Two new features are created: FamilySize (SibSp + Parch + 1) and IsAlone (boolean flag: FamilySize == 1). Social group dynamics are strongly correlated with survival probability.

FamilySize = SibSp + Parch + 1IsAlone = (FamilySize == 1).astype(int)

7

Column pruning

Name, Ticket, and Cabin are dropped. Name is consumed by title extraction, Ticket has high cardinality with no clear signal, and Cabin is 77% missing in training data.

drop(["Name", "Ticket", "Cabin"], axis=1)

8

Model training — Random Forest

A RandomForestClassifier is trained on the full training split. 300 estimators with max_depth=7 balances variance reduction and overfitting risk. Ensemble decision rules are learned across bootstrapped samples and random feature subsets.

n_estimators=300max_depth=7random_state=42

9

Prediction & submission export

The trained model predicts survival (0 or 1) for all 418 test passengers. Predictions are paired with PassengerId and exported as a Kaggle-compatible CSV.

model.predict(test_processed)to_csv("submission.csv", index=False)
